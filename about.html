Why 4th Wall?

We believe experiences should be more than seen.

4th Wall is built around the four senses that shape how we experience the world: what we see, hear, touch and taste.

And then there's the theatre meaning behind our name: breaking the fourth wall.

It's the moment the invisible boundary between the audience and the experience disappears.

That's what we aim to do with every experience we create.

We create experiences that don't leave people standing on the outside looking in. We bring brands, people, spaces, content, performance and interaction together to create something people can genuinely experience.

Because the strongest experiences aren't simply seen.

They are felt.

Our Approach

Our Approach

Start with the organisation, not the event.

Before we talk about stages, décor or guest lists, we want to understand what the organisation needs the experience to achieve. That gives every creative and production decision a reason.

From strategy and creative to planning and on-site execution, we keep the work connected. You get one team looking at the whole picture, rather than a collection of suppliers solving separate pieces.

What We Believe

Good experiences are planned. Great ones are joined up.

The best work happens when the client and agency are solving the same problem together. We are direct about budgets, realistic about timelines and involved in the details that can make or break delivery.

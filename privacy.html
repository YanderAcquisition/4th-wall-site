Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you deal with 4th Wall Experiential.

Last updated: 4 September 2026

4th Wall Experiential (“4th Wall”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and in the course of our work, why we collect it, who we share it with and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written to align with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

4th Wall Experiential is an experiential events agency based in Gauteng, South Africa. We provide strategy, creative and event execution services to brands and organisations. For the purposes of POPIA we are the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy.

You can reach us about anything in this policy at samkelo@4thwallexp.co.za.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and to deliver the work.

  • Information you give us directly. When you complete our contact form we collect your name, e-mail address, company, country and the content of your message. When you subscribe to our updates we collect your e-mail address.
  • Information you give us during a project. Client and supplier contact details, delegate or guest details supplied to us for registration and event management, and correspondence relating to the work.
  • Technical information. Standard information your browser sends when you visit the site, such as IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you view. This is used to keep the site working and secure.

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we do not collect special personal information through this website.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare proposals, timelines and costings.
  • To plan, manage and deliver events, including registration, accreditation, catering and access requirements.
  • To manage our relationship with clients, suppliers and venues, including invoicing.
  • To send updates about our work where you have asked to receive them.
  • To meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

4. Our lawful basis

We process personal information where you have consented, where it is necessary to conclude or perform a contract with you, where the law requires it, or where it serves our legitimate interests in running and protecting the business. Where we rely on consent, such as for marketing e-mails, you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where it is needed to do the work, with:

  • Clients, where you are attending or registering for an event we are delivering on their behalf.
  • Suppliers and partners who form part of delivery, such as venues, caterers, production and technical crew, registration and accreditation providers, and travel providers.
  • Service providers who support our business, including hosting, e-mail and accounting providers.
  • Authorities, where we are required to disclose information by law.

We require anyone acting on our behalf to keep personal information confidential and to process it only on our instructions.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website uses only what is needed to serve pages and remember basic preferences. If we introduce analytics or marketing cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires us to. Enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as there is an active or prospective relationship. Records connected to a completed project are retained for the periods required by South African tax and company law. Marketing subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access and misuse, including access controls and limiting internal access to those who need it. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct, update or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive or out of date.
  • Object to processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing e-mails at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, e-mail samkelo@4thwallexp.co.za. We may need to verify your identity before we act on a request.

10. Information sent outside South Africa

Some of the service providers we use may store information outside South Africa. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the information is subject to a level of protection comparable to POPIA.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The current version will always be published on this page with the date it was last updated.

12. Contact us

For any question, request or complaint about how we handle personal information:

4th Wall Experiential
Gauteng, South Africa
samkelo@4thwallexp.co.za

You can also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe we have not dealt with your concern properly.

The Soil

We manage The Soil. The bookings, the diary, the brand partnerships, the shows, and the launch of their own MCC.

Client
The Soil
Relationship
Artist management since 2024
Scope
Bookings, partnerships, production, product launch

Overview

4th Wall has managed The Soil since 2024. We run the diary and the bookings, negotiate and contract the work, manage the brand partnerships and build the production around the shows that carry their name.

The booking side is the part people see. A promoter or a brand comes to us, and we work out whether the show is right, what it should cost, what the technical rider needs and how it gets delivered on the day.

Because we also produce, we only quote on what we know we can deliver. The people negotiating the fee are the same people who will be standing side of stage.

Managing an artist is not the same as booking one. It means being responsible for the whole career, not just the next show.

Everything around the music.

The launch

Joy Nectar MCC

The work we are proudest of is Joy Nectar MCC, a product we helped The Soil bring to market and sell. It started with a question about what an artist brand can be beyond ticket sales and streaming, and ended with a bottle on the shelf.

We built the launch around the group itself: an in-store activation with tastings, a cocktail bar and The Soil performing live between the wine shelves. Joy Nectar is now stocked exclusively at Norman Goodfellows nationwide.

That is the argument for management and production sitting in the same building. The people who know the artist are the people building the thing being sold.

Marsh

Where business gets done. Conferencing and exhibition work for Marsh, handled end to end.

Client
Marsh
Discipline
Conferencing and exhibitions
Scope
End to end, event after event

Overview

Marsh do not brief us on a piece of an event. They brief us on the event, and we do everything from there.

We handle their conferencing and exhibition work from start to finish: the stage and set, registration, delegate flow, screen content and live camera, branding and signage, the suppliers and the delivery on the day.

Because it is the same team each time, the delegate experience stays consistent from one event to the next while the content changes. We already know how the room behaves, so the planning starts further along than it would with a new supplier every year.

A summit has two audiences in the same room: the programme on stage, and the business being done between sessions.

In the room

Built for both audiences

Rounds instead of rows, a registration desk people can actually find, and breaks with somewhere to stand. Those decisions do as much work as the stage does.

Side screens carry a live camera feed so the speakers stay legible from the back of the room, and the seating keeps sightlines to the stage clear while still letting delegates talk to the people beside them.

Joyous Celebration

Thirty years, a national tour, and every part of it run from one place.

Client
Joyous Celebration
Project
30 Year Celebration Tour
Scope
End to end, every show

Overview

Joyous Celebration is the clearest example of what we mean by end to end. There is no part of the show we are not responsible for.

We plan the events, book the venues, set the ticket prices and sell the tickets. We run access control on the day, manage the suppliers, handle the branding and build the marketing events that sell the shows.

When something moves, it moves in one place. There is no handover between the team that booked the venue and the team working the door.

From the first venue booking to the last ticket scanned at the door, it all runs through one team.

This year

Thirty years on the road

This year that meant the thirty year celebration tour, taken around the country. A tour multiplies every problem a single event has. Each city brings a different venue, a different supplier base, different compliance requirements and a different audience to sell to.

Ticketing and access control are where that pressure usually shows. Pricing has to reflect what each city will carry, and the door has to move a full house without a queue. Both decisions sit with the team that booked the venue, which is exactly why they hold up.

Thirty years is a milestone an audience turns up for. Our job was to make sure nothing about the logistics got in the way of it.

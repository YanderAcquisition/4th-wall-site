What We Do

From the first brief to the final guest

4th Wall Experiential brings strategy, creative thinking and event execution together under one roof. We start with what the organisation needs to achieve, then build the experience around it.

That means fewer handovers, clearer accountability and a team that stays close to the detail from the first conversation through to delivery.

Why 4th Wall?

We believe experiences should be more than seen.

4th Wall is built around the four senses that shape how we experience the world: what we see, hear, touch and taste.

And then there's the theatre meaning behind our name: breaking the fourth wall.

It's the moment the invisible boundary between the audience and the experience disappears.

That's what we aim to do with every experience we create.

Break the wall. Bring people in.
